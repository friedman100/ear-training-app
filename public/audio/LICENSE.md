# Piano samples

Acoustic Grand Piano samples from FluidR3 GM, distributed through
https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts/tree/gh-pages/FluidR3_GM/acoustic_grand_piano-mp3

Original soundfont: Fluid (R3) General MIDI SoundFont, by Frank Wen and contributors.
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Notes C3–C7 are included as original MP3 samples. Playback uses the original pitch,
with a gain envelope to end each note at 0.9 seconds.
